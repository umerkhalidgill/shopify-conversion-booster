# Shopify CRO Guide

## What is CRO?

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) is the process of increasing the percentage of users who take action (buy, add to cart, etc.).

---

## Key Strategies

- Use urgency (timers, low stock)
- Add trust badges
- Optimize product pages
- Improve mobile UX
- Reduce friction in checkout

---

## Expected Results

• +20% to +80% Conversion Rate  
• +15% to +40% AOV
